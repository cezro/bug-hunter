## 2. Technical Finding Details
MegaStore released an Easter promotional campaign offering a single-use coupon code `EASTER25` granting a $25 credit on orders over $50.

Using HTTP/2 multiplexing via Turbo Intruder, 30 concurrent `POST /api/v2/cart/apply-voucher` requests were dispatched within a single TCP window. Due to lack of row locking (`SELECT FOR UPDATE`) on the `user_vouchers` database table, 4 of the requests succeeded before the `used` flag flipped to `true`.

This yielded a total unauthorized discount of $100 instead of the intended $25 limit.

---

## 3. Post-Mortem: Why Was This Duplicate?

### 1. High Visibility Surface
MegaStore announced the Easter promo via social media and push notifications at 08:00 UTC. Hundreds of bounty hunters monitor brand marketing channels to immediately target newly introduced promotion codes.

### 2. Low Exploit Complexity
Testing race conditions on voucher codes requires zero specialized knowledge of the target's internal business logic. It is a standard automated check for top-tier hunters running automated monitors.

### 3. Submission Delay
Discovery occurred at 13:00 UTC, followed by an hour of manual testing and another 20 minutes drafting formatting and video PoCs. By the time the report was submitted at 14:22 UTC, three other researchers had already submitted reports for the exact same voucher code.

---

## 4. Tactical Takeaways & Operational Changes

1. **Avoid Surface-Level Marketing Races:** Do not compete on generic discount vouchers on public programs unless the mechanism is tied to unique internal business workflows (e.g., gift card balance exchanges, currency arbitrage, loyalty tier recalculations).
2. **Accelerated PoC Pipeline:** For fast-moving bugs, write a lean, text-based reproduction with curl/HTTP logs rather than spending an hour recording video PoCs.
3. **Focus on Deep Authentication & Access Control:** High-severity access control defects (like KB-2026-001) yield 4x higher bounties and suffer less than 5% duplication rates compared to 60%+ duplicate rates on marketing promotional codes.
